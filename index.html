THE RESEARCH

Building a more connected
service experience.

An academic investigation into how complementary technologies could support more transparent, accessible, and coordinated microfinance and welfare processes in Sri Lanka.

✳ RESEARCH OVERVIEW

From fragmented workflows to a joined-up platform.

Microfinance and welfare services can involve multiple stakeholders, eligibility checks, financial records, and resident enquiries. When information and processes are fragmented, coordinating decisions and explaining outcomes can become difficult. SmartGrama proposes a unified research prototype that brings together secure record evidence, configurable eligibility rules, predictive decision support, and multilingual information access.

The project is framed as a research and development effort. Its expected benefits will need to be assessed through implementation, testing, and evaluation rather than assumed in advance.

Research problem

Potential fragmentation of records, manual verification, limited auditability, and language barriers in local service workflows.

Research motivation

Explore whether secure, intelligent, and accessible digital workflows can better support residents and authorised officers.

C

Research approach

Develop and evaluate four connected components as part of a single proposed platform.

01 / BACKGROUND

Why this research matters

Welfare programmes and community microfinance can help households access assistance and financial services. At local administrative level, applications may require identity, household, eligibility, and repayment information to be reviewed across different roles. Digital support must balance process efficiency with privacy, accountability, fairness, and understandable communication.

02 / PROPOSED SOLUTION

Four components, one research direction

SmartGrama brings together a blockchain distributed data layer, smart-contract eligibility automation, an AI-based microfinance prediction engine, and an NLP multilingual communication system. Each component investigates a distinct problem while being designed to contribute to the wider platform.

EXPECTED BENEFITS

Designed to support better-informed services.

Potential benefits include improved record traceability, more consistent rule application, additional repayment-risk insights, easier access to information in Sinhala and English, and clearer coordination between system components. These are anticipated benefits, not claims of experimentally proven outcomes.

EXPLAINABLE SUPPORT
01

Secure record lifecycle

Relevant records are prepared and validated before verification evidence is recorded. A careful design should keep sensitive personal information off a public or broadly replicated ledger, using access controls and references or hashes where appropriate.

02

Eligibility and decision support

Configurable rules can help assess whether an application meets stated criteria. The design should make the applicable rules and reasons reviewable, handle incomplete data safely, and avoid treating an automated output as an unquestionable final decision.

03

Microfinance prediction

A machine-learning model can estimate a defined outcome from suitable historical data. Evaluation should consider data quality, class balance, calibration, fairness, privacy, and whether the prediction is useful to trained reviewers.

04

Multilingual knowledge access

The NLP component can identify a question, retrieve relevant passages from an approved knowledge collection, and generate a concise answer grounded in those passages. Sinhala and English support should be tested for retrieval quality and answer faithfulness.

FOUR RESEARCH COMPONENTS

Different disciplines.
A shared purpose.

The research is divided into focused components, each addressing a complementary part of the proposed SmartGrama platform.

◇COMPONENT 01

Blockchain Distributed Data Layer

Investigates secure, verifiable, and auditable records for welfare and microfinance workflows, including a hybrid approach that separates sensitive personal data from on-chain verification evidence.
